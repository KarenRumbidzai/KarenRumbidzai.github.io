AI as a Teammate: Who's Working on This Ticket?

An AI agent just picked up a Jira ticket. Now what? This three-part series shows how a TPM brings an AI coding agent onto a delivery team. Part 1 sets its role, access and house rules. Part 2 maps the journey from Jira ticket to GitHub pull request. Part 3 runs a worked example, including what happens when the ticket leaves a gap. The agent does the legwork. Humans keep the final say.
